# The cube (main site's travelling element)

Decided with Tejal, 2026-10-17. The cube starts from one fixed scramble, and a hardcoded, verified solve plays as the visitor scrolls. It is solved by the end of the site.

## Files
- `blender/exports/cube.glb` (15 KB), generated by `blender/cube.py`:
  - one `cubie_body` (edge 1.0, rounded) and one `sticker`, which sits on the cubie's +Y face in three's axes;
  - instance 27 cubies at 1.02 spacing, and copy and rotate the sticker onto each outer face.
- `docs/cube/solve.json`: the scramble, 8 stages, 44 moves (it was 62 until 2026-11).
- `docs/cube/verify.py`: replays the solve and checks every stage. Port it as a unit test for the site's cube engine; the engine must pass it.
- `docs/cube/search.py`: how solve.json was found (`python3 -I search.py --write`). Built backwards from solved out of standard pieces (a 2-look OLL, an A perm, F2L inserts on U and the slot's own faces); kept only if the cross and every pair are optimal, no pair could have gone in quicker, nothing is done before its stage, and the start looks like a real scramble.

## Notation and colours
- White on D (bottom), yellow on U (top), green F (front), red R, orange L, blue B.
- Standard notation: a turn is clockwise as seen looking at that face; `'` is anticlockwise, and `2` is a half turn.
- Load the page with the scramble already applied (instantly). Never show the scramble being played.

## Scroll → moves
- Each chapter's moves are spread evenly over that chapter's scroll length. Crossing a move's threshold plays that quarter turn with a spring (about 180 ms).
- Scrolling back plays the inverse moves.
- Fast scrolling queues the moves and plays them faster, so the cube never skips a state.
- Caption: a small label beside the cube when a stage completes (the captions above), plus a move counter ("move 23 / 44"), set in Host Grotesk, 12 px (see the reference page).

## Constant motion and presence (Tejal: constant motion, reacting when the visitor comes close)
- **Always alive:** a slow tumble and a gentle float, so it is never still. Each chapter defines a home spot in the margins, never over text.
- **Comes to the visitor:** if the cursor rests in empty space (not over text, media, links or inputs) for about 0.6 s, the cube glides towards it with a spring. It stops about 120 px away, turns to face the cursor, and never sits under the cursor. When the cursor moves onto content, the cube goes back home.
- **Near the cursor (within about 160 px):** it leans towards the cursor and slows its tumble. The face under the cursor highlights, and the cursor shows a twist hint.
- **Phones:** no following. The cube sits in a corner, and a tap twists a face.

## Clicks (Tejal, 2026-11: your twists stay)
- Clicking a face turns it a quarter turn clockwise (Shift: anticlockwise).
- The twist stays. Nothing is ever undone: no undo animation, no stack. Scroll keeps playing the solve's moves (or their inverses, going back) on top of whatever state the cube is in, so a cube you've twisted won't come out solved at the end. That's intended.
- Milestones only tell the truth: "two layers done" shows only if the first two layers really are solved when the solve gets there, and "you solved it!" (with the celebration) fires the moment the cube really is solved, whether the solve got it there or you did it by hand. Once it's really solved it rests: scrolling back up doesn't scramble it again (a twist still turns it).
- If a click arrives mid-twist, queue it until the current twist settles. Never drop input.

## Materials per chapter
Classic sticker colours on black plastic in every chapter, as in `decisions/reference-a0.html`. No material effects.

## Reduced motion
No tumble and no following. Moves apply instantly at their thresholds, and clicks still work (instantly).
